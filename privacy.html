Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

FitnessGainer is designed to protect your privacy. The app does not require a user account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, fitness logs, nutrition entries, or body metrics to external servers. All data you enter stays securely on your local device.

2. Third-Party Services

We use official Apple In-App Purchases to process subscription payments. Apple manages this payment data securely under their own privacy policy. We do not have access to your credit card information or banking details.

3. Changes to This Policy

Our commitment to your privacy is permanent. Because this app operates entirely on your local device without user accounts, our data collection practices will not change. If any future updates change how the app functions, this policy will be updated immediately alongside that release to ensure complete transparency.

If you have any questions, please contact us at: support.fitnessgainer@gmail.com
